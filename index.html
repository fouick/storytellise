A dystopian novel by E.F. Wick
Every e-Mortal is now entitled to pick their ideal future on their fifteenth birthday.
With advances in Research and AI, Skills and Knowledge are globally centralised and equally shared. No more shady jobby selections, no more gruelling diseases, no more painful heartbreaks.
Only bliss and tokens.
And should anyone not be fully happy with the life selected, they can always switch at will, Satisfaction guaranteed*.

*Conditions apply...


18 April a.m. – A deluge of bad news is washing over the Bow.
Berlin is under water, Dirdam burst into flames in Spain, and the pyramids in Oriac threaten to be engulfed.
It falls to Penny to lead an epic intercontinental rescue mission. She has 24 hours to devise her strategy and bring the HemiNor’s populations to safety.
But how to assist millions when no Ambassador answers?

_


Dive into this multilayered plot and piece together the puzzle of an incredible conspiracy.
Travel at the whim of the tides and the wavy stock market, from the French metro corridors to the tarmac in Egypt.
Let yourself be swept away by this biting reflection on the place of free will and identity in a fully digital world.


A story rooted in transhumanism, bioethics and inclusive diversity, sprinkled with a generous dose of totalitarian power.
For Divergent and Silo readers who grew up with Black Mirror.

Behind the keyboard

A self-taught author and publisher, E.F. Wick developed a passion at a very young age for dystopian worlds, intertwined destinies, conspiracies and the environmental risks inherent in new technologies.

Their personal adventures and setbacks in SaaS startups and the Entertainment industry inspired them to write their first science fiction novel, ScarCity.
